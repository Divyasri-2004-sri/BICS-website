About the Summit
Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 (BiCS 2026) is a premier international summit bringing together global leaders, policymakers, scientists, academicians, innovators, investors, youth leaders, environmentalists, and industry experts to address the world's most pressing climate and ocean-related challenges.

Centered on the intersection of climate intelligence, marine conservation, sustainable technologies, and the blue economy, BiCS 2026 serves as a collaborative platform for transformative dialogue, innovation, and action.

The summit is designed as an action-oriented incubator where youth innovation, academic excellence, policy leadership, and industrial expertise converge to co-create scalable AI-driven solutions, strengthen maritime sustainability, accelerate climate resilience, and secure sustainable blue economies aligned with SDG 14 — Life Below Water.

BiCS 2026 serves as an action-oriented platform where innovation, policy, science, and sustainability converge to accelerate global ocean climate solutions.

Summit Vision

"Smart Oceans. Sustainable Future."
To establish BiCS 2026 as a globally recognized platform advancing science-based climate action, ocean stewardship, and sustainable development through collaboration between governments, academia, industry, and communities.

Mission

Mission Objectives
Driving innovation for ocean and climate sustainability through strategic global collaboration.
Promote Blue Economy & Ocean Sustainability
Advance sustainable blue economy strategies that support ocean conservation, coastal development, and long-term economic resilience.
Strengthen Climate Resilience & Adaptation Strategies
Support adaptation and resilience strategies for vulnerable coastal ecosystems and communities affected by climate change.
Encourage Innovation, Research & Technology Transfer
Promote research, technological innovation, and knowledge exchange in marine science, fisheries, AI, and maritime technologies.
Support Youth & Student Engagement in Climate Leadership
Engage students and young innovators in climate leadership, sustainability initiatives, and ocean stewardship.
Facilitate Policy Dialogue & International Partnerships
Enable international collaboration and policy dialogue among governments, academia, industries, NGOs, and global institutions.
Showcase Scalable Sustainability Solutions & Green Investments
Highlight scalable sustainability solutions, climate technologies, and green investment opportunities for the future blue economy.

Venue

Host City & Venue
Why Visakhapatnam?

Visakhapatnam is one of India's emerging coastal innovation hubs, strategically located along the Bay of Bengal and uniquely positioned for global dialogue on ocean sustainability and climate resilience.

The city offers world-class convention infrastructure, beachfront accessibility, marine research institutions, and strong maritime connectivity — making it an ideal destination for an international climate summit.

Why Radisson Blu?

Radisson Blu Resort, Visakhapatnam, reflects a strong commitment to sustainable tourism and environmentally responsible hospitality practices.

The venue combines world-class conference infrastructure with eco-conscious operations focused on reducing carbon emissions, energy consumption, and water usage — aligning perfectly with the vision of BiCS 2026.
