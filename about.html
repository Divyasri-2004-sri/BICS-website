About BiCS 2026

Everything you need to know about the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 — its vision, mission, themes, program, and global impact.

Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 (BiCS 2026) is an international flagship summit designed to bring together global leaders, policymakers, scientists, academicians, innovators, investors, youth leaders, environmentalists, and industry experts to address the most pressing climate and ocean-related challenges of our time.

The summit focuses on integrating blue economy strategies, climate intelligence, sustainable technologies, marine conservation, and global partnerships to create impactful, scalable solutions for the future.

BiCS 2026 focuses on integrating Artificial Intelligence, marine science, blue economy strategies, sustainable technologies, climate intelligence, and youth innovation to build scalable solutions for a resilient future.

Date & Venue
  • Dates: 2nd & 3rd October 2026
  • Location: Visakhapatnam, India
Organized By
  • Project Planner – EventsKart
  • Co-Partner – GOAEC
Vision
To establish BiCS 2026 as a globally recognized platform driving science-based climate action, ocean stewardship, and sustainable development, while fostering collaboration between governments, academia, private sector, and communities.
Mission Objectives
  • Promote Blue Economy & Ocean Sustainability — Advance sustainable blue economy strategies that support ocean conservation, coastal development, and long-term economic resilience.
  • Strengthen Climate Resilience & Adaptation — Support adaptation and resilience strategies for vulnerable coastal ecosystems and communities affected by climate change.
  • Encourage Innovation, Research & Technology Transfer — Promote research, technological innovation, and knowledge exchange in marine science, fisheries, AI, and maritime technologies.
  • Support Youth & Student Engagement — Engage students and young innovators in climate leadership, sustainability initiatives, and ocean stewardship.
  • Facilitate Policy Dialogue & Partnerships — Enable international collaboration and policy dialogue among governments, academia, industries, NGOs, and global institutions.
  • Showcase Scalable Solutions & Green Investments — Highlight scalable sustainability solutions, climate technologies, and green investment opportunities for the future blue economy.

  • Decarbonization of shipping industry
  • Smart ports and coastal infrastructure

Host City & Venue
Why Visakhapatnam?

Visakhapatnam is India's emerging coastal innovation hub, strategically positioned on the Bay of Bengal, making it an ideal venue for a summit centered on ocean sustainability and climate resilience.

The selected venue offers large-scale convention capacity and beachfront access suitable for international conferences.

Why at Radisson Blue?

Radisson Blu and the broader Radisson Hotel Group are highly recognized for their commitment to sustainable tourism and eco-friendly hospitality.

The brand has championed environmental initiatives for decades and focuses heavily on reducing carbon, energy, and water consumption.
