Accommodation & Logistics

Blue Radisson Hotel, Visakhapatnam

We can try to help the participant with a comfortable accommodation package at the venue — by Blue Radisson Hotel by the end of June 2026.

This package includes food. Arrivals should be on 1st Oct by 3 p.m, whereas check-in is allowed by 3 p.m onwards.

Important Note

  • If Participant books the accommodation at the Venue — Blue Radisson Hotel, food is included from 1st Oct 2026 dinner to Breakfast on 4th Oct 2026, as a complete combo pack, including accommodation.
  • Accommodation booking link will be posted here in the last week of June 2026 — Single & Double Sharing options available.
  • We can also share a low budget accommodation package nearby the summit venue by the end of June 2026.
