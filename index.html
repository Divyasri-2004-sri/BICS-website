The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 aims to bring together policymakers, researchers, industry leaders, and coastal community stakeholders to promote sustainable ocean resource management aligned with SDG 14 (Life Below Water).

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BiCS) 2026 is a premier 2-day event uniting advanced technology and marine conservation. BiCS 2026 serves as an action-oriented incubator where youth innovation, academic rigor, and industrial operations converge to co-create scalable AI solutions, decarbonize global shipping, and secure sustainable blue economies.

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 is an action-oriented, cross-sector event designed to pioneer data-driven solutions for ocean climate action. Overview

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 is an action-oriented, cross-sector event designed to pioneer data-driven solutions for ocean climate action. By bridging the gap between advanced technology and marine conservation, the summit serves as a collaborative incubator where young talent, academic rigor, and industrial operations converge to build a sustainable blue economy.

Purpose

The objectives of the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BiCS) 2026 are centred on the synergy between marine preservation and technological innovation.

Encourage innovation in marine technology and fisheries
Strengthen maritime safety and satellite-based solutions
Facilitate policy dialogue and industry collaboration
Support coastal community development and livelihoods
