For Students (18–25 years) — Poster Presentation

Theme: Blue Intelligence For A Sustainable Ocean

The Smart Port Challenge
Shipping Logistics
The co-Pulse Track
Eco-Tourism App
The Civic Blueprint
Coastal Policy
A. Technical Specifications
  • Size: Standard A0 (841 × 1189 mm), Vertical/Portrait orientation
  • Format: PDF or High-Res JPEG (min 300 DPI)
  • Language: English
  • Word Limit: Max 300 words
B. Content Requirements
  • Title & Team: Project name, participant names, institution
  • The Challenge: Problem related to Blue Economy/Climate
  • The Solution: Intelligence-based approach (Tech, Policy, or Community)
  • SDG Alignment: Mention SDG (4, 7, 9, 13, 14, or 17)
  • Visuals: Charts, maps, or infographics — minimize long text
Steps to Create a Submission (Student Team of 2)
1
Form Your Team — Gather a team of 2 eligible students. Cross-disciplinary teams (e.g., Data Science + Marine Biology) are highly encouraged.
2
Select Your Track — Smart Port Challenge, co-Pulse Track, or The Civic Blueprint.
3
Submit Registration — Fill out the student portal form with team details, GitHub profile links, and college identity proofs before 10th July 2026 (without late fee). Late fee deadline: 3rd August 2026.
4
Attend the Launch — Register between 14:00–17:00 hrs to receive registration kits and poster presentation timelines.
