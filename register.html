Call for Papers & Posters

Register for BiCS 2026

We are pleased to announce the Call for Papers and Posters for the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026, scheduled for 2nd & 3rd October 2026. The summit explores the intersection of Blue Intelligence & Economy, focusing on SDGs 4, 7, 9, 13, 14, and 17.

Why You Should Apply

Join the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026 and be part of a transformative global conversation on ocean sustainability, technology, and policy.

  • Win the "Blue Innovator Award 2026" for top poster at the Valedictory Ceremony
  • Cash award is applicable for the title winner.
  • Paricipation certifcate + Title Winner Certificate.
  • Network at the heart of India's Innovation Ecosystem.
  • Flash Pitch : Participants must be prepared for a 3-minute pitch to jury team, Interantional Deligations, Keynote Speakers and other guest.(During Break).

A. Technical Specifications
  • Size: Standard A0 (841 × 1189 mm), Vertical/Portrait orientation
  • Format: PDF or High-Res JPEG (min 300 DPI)
  • Language: English
  • Word Limit: Max 300 words
B. Content Requirements
  • Title & Team: Project name, participant names, institution
  • The Challenge: Problem related to Blue Economy/Climate
  • The Solution: Intelligence-based approach (Tech, Policy, or Community)
  • SDG Alignment: Mention SDG (4, 7, 9, 13, 14, or 17)
  • Visuals: Charts, maps, or infographics — minimize long text
