Submissions

Explore the Core Themes, Program Highlights, and Featured Research Topics for BiCS 2026.

Topics for Students

Student teams of 2 are eligible. Cross-disciplinary teams are highly encouraged.

1

The Smart Port Challenge

Shipping Logistics — tackle real-world port efficiency and emissions challenges using AI and data.

2

The co-Pulse Track

Eco-Tourism App — design tech-driven solutions to promote sustainable coastal tourism.

3

The Civic Blueprint

Coastal Policy — develop community-centered frameworks that protect fragile coastal ecosystems.

Steps to Create a Submission
1
Form Your Team

Gather a team of 2 eligible students. Cross-disciplinary teams (e.g., Data Science paired with Marine Biology) are highly encouraged.

2
Select Your Track

Choose from The Smart Port Challenge (Shipping Logistics), The co-Pulse Track (Eco-Tourism App), or The Civic Blueprint (Coastal Policy).

3
Submit Registration

Fill out the student portal form with your team details, active GitHub profile links, and college identity proofs before 10th July 2026 (without late fee). If late fee, before 3rd Aug 2026.

4
Attend the Launch

Register between 14:00–17:00 hrs to receive your registration kit and poster presentation timelines, if selected for the summit. Once registered with fee details, you will receive an email with poster and paper submission details within 2 service days.

Poster Presentation Guidelines (Students 18–25 years)

Theme: Blue Intelligence For A Sustainable Ocean

A. Technical Specifications

  • Size: Standard A0 (841 × 1189 mm) — Vertical/Portrait orientation
  • Format: PDF / High-Res JPEG (min 300 DPI)
  • Language: English

B. Content Requirements

Every poster must include the following sections:

1Title & Team: Name of the project, participant names, and institution.
2The Challenge: What specific problem related to the Blue Economy/Climate are you solving?
3The Solution: Describe your "Intelligence" based approach (Tech, Policy, or Community action).
4SDG Alignment: Explicitly mention which SDG (4, 7, 9, 13, 14, or 17) your project supports.
5Visuals: Use charts, maps, or infographics. Minimize long paragraphs of text.

C. Hackathon Selection Process

  • Student: Max 300 words
  • Abstract Submission Last Date: 31st July 2026, 9 PM IST / 11:30 AM NYT
  • Late Fee Abstract Submission: 10th August 2026, 9 PM IST / 11:30 AM NYT
  • Select a Summit Track from: AI & Ocean Intelligence | Decarbonization | Offshore Energy | Resilience & Just Transition

Why You Should Apply?

Win the title award — "Blue Innovator Award 2026" for top 1 poster during the Valedictory Ceremony.
Network at the heart of India's Innovation Ecosystem.
Cash Awards and International Valid Certificates.
Flash Pitch: Participants must be prepared for a 3-minute pitch to judges (including UN Delegation) during Tea Breaks.
