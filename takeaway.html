Summit Takeaways

Discover the key benefits, awards, and transformative opportunities available to every participant at the Blue Intelligence Climate Summit 2026.

Awards

Cash Prize for Student Innovators

Multiple technical tracks challenging students to solve real-world industrial and civic problems.

Cash Prize for Publications

Top research papers fast-tracked into prestigious journals like Marine Policy and Applied Ocean Research.

High-Value Sponsorships

Corporate-backed tiers — Blue Whale, Blue Shark, Blue Dolphin, Blue Reefs — driving direct industry alignment and recruitment pipelines.

Takeaways For Participants
1
Campus to Coast

Activating young minds and researchers to transition theoretical marine science and data prototypes into actionable real-world applications.

2
Industrial Transition (Shipping & Industrialists)

Deploying AI-driven logistics and route optimization tools to rapidly lower emissions and support cleaner maritime supply chains.

3
Resilient Economies (Local Govt & Eco-Tourism)

Empowering public officials and local hospitality businesses with data to build policies that safeguard fragile coastal ecosystems while driving sustainable local tourism.

4
Awards

Top 1 poster of one Student will receive the Cash Award 2026. Top 1 paper presentation of one Researcher will receive the Cash Award 2026. Surprise "Cash Awards" or "Cash Vouchers" from various industries.

5
Student Track Winners

Multiple technical tracks challenging students to solve real-world industrial and civic problems. Top 3 will be awarded as Title Winners.

6
Research Publication Winners

Top research papers fast-tracked into prestigious journals like Marine Policy and Applied Ocean Research. Top 3 will be awarded as Title Winners.
