About the Summit
About BiCS 2026

The Blue Intelligence Climate Summit (BiCS) 2026 is a premier international platform connecting climate science, marine conservation, artificial intelligence, and sustainable blue economy innovation. The summit brings together governments, academia, industry leaders, startups, researchers, and youth to co-create scalable solutions for ocean resilience and climate action.

Summit Vision
"Smart Oceans. Sustainable Future."
Driving data-driven ocean climate solutions through the synergy of technology, policy, sustainability, and community collaboration.
Cross-Sector Collaboration
Governments, UN-linked institutions, universities, NGOs, startups, and industry leaders united under one platform for ocean action.
Research & Innovation
Selected papers fast-tracked to Scopus-indexed journals including Marine Policy and Applied Ocean Research.
SDG Focus
Aligned with SDGs 4, 7, 9, 13, 14 and 17 — integrating AI, Marine Science, and Maritime Logistics.
Youth Leadership
A dedicated Youth Innovation Forum empowering the next generation of ocean scientists and climate leaders.

Why Visakhapatnam?

Visakhapatnam is one of India's emerging coastal innovation hubs, strategically located on the Bay of Bengal and ideal for global dialogue on ocean sustainability, climate resilience, and blue economy transformation.

With its thriving port, marine research institutions, and growing technology ecosystem, Visakhapatnam offers the perfect convergence of maritime heritage and future-forward innovation.
